LCARS TERMINAL OPERATION INSTRUCTIONS
1. Initiate a stellar sector scan by clicking the SCAN (track_changes) controller in the upper-right panel.
2. Telemetry will compile a tactical orbital chart. Click on stars, planets, or moons to zoom in or query sensor arrays.
3. Toggle the data readout panels below to view detailed Stellar Groups, Planetary Tables, and Points of Interest.
4. Activate the MENU (menu) panel to import (load) or export (save) system data matrices.
